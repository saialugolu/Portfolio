The Journey So Far

Understanding customers has always been at the center of my career.

My career started in Customer Success, where I learned that understanding users goes far beyond resolving support requests. It is about understanding their goals, finding the real problem behind a question, setting clear expectations, and helping customers achieve meaningful outcomes.

Over time, I became increasingly interested in how products are designed, why users behave the way they do, how features are researched and built, and how the right product reaches the right audience.

Today, my experience spans Customer Success, product operations, product analytics, PRDs, user stories, SQL, SEO and AEO content strategy, B2B outreach, ICP development, lead generation, and growth experimentation. As a Growth Product Manager, I connect customer insights with product strategy, collaborate across teams, and validate ideas through experimentation.
